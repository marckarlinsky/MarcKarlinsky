      Notes, definitions and what is left out
      Sources
      2026 District 4A results by precinct are the Board of Election Commissioners' precinct export for Subdistrict 4A (file dated November 6, 2026). The 2026 Board president votes for the same precincts come from the Board's results feed. The 2024 District 4 results are the Board's precinct export for that year's race. None of this is a certified canvass.
      Shares
      A share is a candidate's votes divided by all votes cast in that contest in that precinct. Margin is one candidate's share minus another's. Votes for write-ins and undervotes are not in the denominators.
      Split precincts
      — precincts are split between 4A and a neighboring district. For 2026 District 4A only the 4A side is counted, and the map draws only that side. The Board president and 2024 numbers are for the whole precinct. Comparisons that mix them are faded on the map and left out of the correlations.
      Same precinct numbers in 2024
      The 2024 file uses the same ward and precinct numbers, and every 4A precinct appears in it. This page assumes those numbers cover the same boundaries in both years. If any precinct lines moved between 2024 and 2026, that precinct's change is not reliable.
      Different fields
      The 2024 race had six candidates in all of District 4, which later became 4A and 4B. The 2026 race had three in 4A. Raw shares are not comparable across the two years, which is why the main comparison uses Zaccor's share of the two-way vote against her main rival. The rival is Rosenfeld in 2024 and Sherratt in 2026.
      Correlations
      A correlation across precincts says that places that voted one way in one contest tended to vote a similar way in another. It does not say the same voters did so.
